## What stands out
- **Format #1 is the arbitrage play.** It broke out on **4+ unrelated small accounts (16–28K)**, so it passes his "3+ unrelated accounts" test. It needs almost no production: 6 seconds, one line of text, value in the caption. That gives a high price for low effort.
- **Small accounts win with short videos (6–11 s).** Big accounts win with long sponsored videos (45–90 s).
- **ethan_r_ng is a textbook outlier.** He has 7.5K followers, his median is ~6.9K views, and one reel got 454K (66×). The format was "AI edited this video" meta content.
